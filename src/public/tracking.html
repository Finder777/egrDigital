What is Mantracking?

Mantracking is the art and science of tracking human beings through their physical traces, behaviors, and environmental interactions. It involves interpreting footprints, broken vegetation, discarded items, and other signs to determine the direction, speed, and activities of a person.

Who are Mantrackers?

Mantracking is used by various fields such as law enforcement, military operations, search and rescue missions, wilderness survivalists and urban trackers.

Why is Mantracking Important?

It enables individuals to locate missing persons, track suspects, and navigate challenging terrains effectively.

Physical Safety whilst tracking

If you decide to deploy to an area to track quarry, then you must not underestimate the danger that you may be exposed to. In particular, if you are manhunting you must determine whether the quarry poses a threat to you and your physical safety. This is especially important when tracking alone.

There are several threats- quarry, competitor trackers/ grey forces, terrain, weather. The ability to appropriately defend yourself is crucial. Experienced and skilled individuals are dangerous to track. See the videos below for a reality check and seek further training to improve your survivability.

REMOTE SURVEILLANCE:

You must evaluate the operating environment before you deploy to an area. You must assess whether tracking is possible given the security situation which includes air threats, enemy forces or other security risks.
